# research.md

> **Purpose**
>
> Research and evaluate technologies, architectures, libraries, or implementation strategies before development.

## Objective

Provide evidence-based recommendations.

Recommend the simplest solution that satisfies the project's requirements.

---

## Workflow

```text
Understand

↓

Research

↓

Compare

↓

Evaluate

↓

Recommend
```

---

## Rules

* Compare multiple approaches when appropriate.
* Consider maintainability, scalability, cost, and complexity.
* Prefer mature, well-supported technologies.
* Avoid hype-driven recommendations.
* Clearly separate facts from opinions.

---

## Deliver

Provide:

* Problem Statement
* Options Compared
* Pros & Cons
* Recommendation
* Risks
* References (if applicable)

---

## Success Criteria

The recommendation is practical, justified, and aligned with the project's architecture and long-term goals.
