What is Metformin generally used for?

smart_toy
verified Verified medicine information

Metformin is an oral medication primarily used to manage type 2 diabetes. It belongs to a class of drugs called biguanides. It works by helping your body respond better to the insulin it makes naturally, decreasing the amount of sugar your liver makes, and decreasing the amount of sugar your intestines absorb.

psychology AI-assisted explanation

Think of it like a traffic controller for sugar in your body. It slows down sugar entering your blood from your food, tells your liver to produce less sugar, and helps your cells take in the sugar that is already circulating. It is often the first medication prescribed for newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes.
